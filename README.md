## ZENITH – AI-Powered Road Safety & Emergency Response System

ZENITH is a smart driver safety and emergency assistance platform designed to reduce road accidents and ensure rapid emergency response during critical situations. The application continuously monitors vehicle speed, location, and sensor data to identify unsafe driving conditions and potential accidents in real time.

## Key Features

## 🚗 Real-Time Speed Monitoring

Tracks vehicle speed using GPS.
Provides visual and audio warnings when the driver exceeds safe speed limits.
Helps prevent overspeed-related accidents.

## ⚠️ Intelligent Crash Detection

Uses motion sensors, G-force analysis, sudden speed changes, and impact detection to identify possible accidents.
Gives the driver a short countdown period to cancel false alerts.

## 📍 Live GPS Tracking & Navigation

Displays the driver's current location and route.
Identifies accident-prone risk zones and alerts drivers to drive cautiously in those areas.

## 🚑 Automated Emergency Response

If a crash is confirmed, Zenith automatically alerts emergency contacts.
Live accident location is shared through SMS and WhatsApp.
Nearby ambulance services are notified, and the first available ambulance can accept the request.

## 🏥 Hospital & Police Coordination

Nearby hospitals within a 5–10 km radius receive essential patient information such as name, age, blood group, and accident location.
Police stations can also be notified to assist with emergency response and traffic management.

## 🗺️ Smart Ambulance Routing

Once an ambulance accepts the request, Zenith provides the fastest route to the accident location and then to the nearest suitable hospital using real-time traffic data.

## 🔧 Roadside Assistance Services

Helps users locate nearby mechanics, service centers, tow trucks, puncture repair shops, fuel stations, and battery assistance services during vehicle breakdowns.

## 📸 Future Enhancements

Automatic accident photo capture.
Audio recording during incidents.
Driver drowsiness detection.
Weather and road hazard alerts.
Trip analytics and driving safety scores.
Conclusion

ZENITH acts as a digital co-driver that not only helps prevent accidents but also coordinates emergency services, medical assistance, and roadside support when accidents or breakdowns occur, ensuring faster response times and improved road safety for drivers.
